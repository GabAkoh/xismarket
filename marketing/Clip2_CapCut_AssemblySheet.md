## Build steps
1. **VO → A1** at 0:00 (sets length, ~55–60s).
2. **MUSIC → A2**, ~15–20% volume (−18 to −20 dB). Fade in 0:00–0:02, fade out last 1.5s.
3. **V1 b-roll** in the 5 blocks, hard-cut on the timecodes, scale/crop to fill 9:16.
4. **Captions (V3):** Captions → Auto-captions → English. White text, thin black outline, lower-center.
5. **Headlines (V2)** from the table, "Pop"/"Rise" in, ≤6 words.
6. **CTA card (0:50–0:60):** solid bg + LOGO + "XISSuite.com".
7. **Transitions:** hard cuts or 4–6 frame dissolves only.

## Polish & export
- **Hook check:** the bin + "This is money. In the bin." must both read by 0:02.
- **Mute test:** story must work with sound off.
- **Loudness:** target −16 LUFS (Audacity → Loudness Normalization if needed).
- **Export:** 1080×1920, 30fps, MP4/H.264 → `D:\xismarket\marketing\renders\XISSuite_Clip2_MoneyInTheBin_v1.mp4`.
