## Build steps (in order)

1. **Drop VO onto A1** starting at 0:00. This sets total length (~55–60s). If it runs long, trim pauses in CapCut; if short, hold the CTA card to reach ~60s.
2. **Add MUSIC to A2**, full length. Set its volume to **~15–20%** (−18 to −20 dB) so it sits *under* the voice. Fade in 0:00–0:02, fade out last 1.5s.
3. **Lay V1 b-roll** in the 5 blocks above, cutting exactly on the timecodes. Scale/crop each clip to fill the 9:16 frame (no black bars).
4. **Captions (V3) — do this automatically:** Toolbar → **Captions → Auto-captions → English**. It transcribes the VO and burns in subtitles. Style: large, high-contrast, white text + thin black outline, positioned lower-center (above the CTA zone).
5. **Add headline overlays (V2)** from the table — these are the big punchy lines, separate from the auto-captions. Use a **Text animation → "Pop" or "Rise"** in, quick. Keep ≤6 words on screen.
6. **Build the CTA card (0:50–0:60):** add a solid color background clip, drop LOGO centered, add "XISSuite.com" text below it, optional subtle fade-in.
7. **Transitions:** keep them minimal — a hard cut or a 4–6 frame dissolve between segments. No flashy wipes; they cheapen it.

---

## Polish pass
- **Hook check:** the first 3 seconds must land. Make sure the SHOP visual + "did you make money?" headline are both visible by 0:01.
- **Mute test:** play the whole thing muted. The captions + headlines alone should tell the story — most viewers never turn sound on.
- **Loudness:** CapCut → export, then check the voice sits around **−16 LUFS**. If CapCut can't, run the final through Audacity (Effect → Loudness Normalization → −16 LUFS).
