# XIS Suite — Vertical Short Clips (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)

Three standalone 60-second clips cut from Episode 01's strongest moments. Each is built to **stop the scroll in the first 3 seconds, deliver one idea, and end on the product.**

**Conventions used below:**
- **9:16 vertical.** Face/voiceover optional — all three work as pure screen-recording + captions if you'd rather not show hosts.
- **[B-ROLL]** = what's on screen. Use your real assets: `XISSuite_Demo.mp4`, `StoreImage.jpg`, `FeaturedCollections.jpg`, `BabyToys.png`, `barcode-label-preview.png`.
- **[TEXT]** = big on-screen caption (burn these in — most people watch muted).
- **[VO]** = spoken line (reuse the same two voices from the episode, or one narrator).
- **[INSERT]** = fill with a real fact before posting. Don't invent. (URLs are already set to **XISSuite.com**.)
- **Timings are targets; total ≤ 60s each.** End every clip on the same CTA card for brand consistency.

## Posting notes
- **Order:** post **Clip 1 first** (widest hook) → **Clip 2** (specific pain) → **Clip 3** (vision/retarget). Space 2–3 days apart.
- **Same CTA card on all three** — builds recognition and makes them feel like a series.
- **Burn in captions** (muted autoplay is the norm). Keep text large and high-contrast.
- **Hook rule:** the first line must land in 3 seconds or the scroll wins. All three open on a provocative question or a visual of loss — that's deliberate.
- **Meta loop:** you already run Pixel tracking — run all three as ads to the same audience and keep whichever hook drives the most sign-ups; that becomes your evergreen ad.
- **A/B the CTA:** try "Watch the free demo" vs. "See your real numbers" and let the Pixel decide.
